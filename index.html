The Challenge

Default Portfolios Are Getting Harder to Manage

Dozens of law firms. Multiple states. Evolving regulations. And the cost of building oversight in-house keeps climbing. CDS is the single layer of accountability across all of it — so you don't have to be.

01

Fragmented Vendor Networks

Multiple law firms and vendors across jurisdictions with inconsistent processes, timelines, and reporting — creating blind spots.

02

An Unforgiving Compliance Environment

OCC consent orders, CFPB enforcement actions, and GSE servicing mandates demand documentation and oversight that most servicers struggle to maintain internally.

03

Cost of Internal Build-Out

Standing up an in-house oversight function requires dedicated staff, technology, and institutional knowledge that takes years to develop.

That's exactly what CDS was built to solve.

Industry Expertise
