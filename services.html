our approach

Oversight That Drives Outcomes

Most servicers work with dozens of law firms and vendors across multiple states — each with different processes, timelines, and reporting standards. CDS sits between you and your vendor network, providing a consistent oversight layer that ensures every file is moving, every deadline is met, and every exception is caught early.

We don't replace your existing vendors — we make them perform better. Our team monitors milestones, flags delays, enforces SLAs, and delivers the reporting you need to stay compliant and in control.

01Assign & Onboard

Every referral is matched to the right vendor and tracked from day one. Files are graded based on type and impediments, with SLAs assigned to each grade.

02Monitor & Manage

Standardized 15- and 30-day follow-ups with counsel and vendors increase resolution velocity and ensure SLA adherence.

03Report & Optimize

Monthly centralized reporting with file grades, aging, attorney performance scorecards, and structured opportunity for firm rebuttals.

Foreclosure Oversight

Fully customizable oversight aligned with each client's operational, compliance, and risk-mitigation objectives. Cradle-to-grave litigation monitoring with attorney performance scorecarding.

  • Attorney matrix oversight with performance scorecarding
  • Contested and uncontested foreclosure timeline management
  • RESPA issues, TROs, and invalid foreclosure claims oversight
  • Billing oversight with milestone validation and AP verification
  • Witness coordination for court and mediation proceedings

Bankruptcy Oversight

A seamless national solution supported by experienced attorneys and paralegals managing bankruptcy matters across entire loan portfolios — including credit card, automobile, and mortgage assets.

  • Nationwide coverage across all 94 U.S. Bankruptcy Courts
  • Proofs of Claim management and compliance review
  • Motions for Relief monitoring and execution
  • Continuous collection of national and local bankruptcy rules
  • Objection monitoring and adversary proceeding oversight

Litigation Oversight

Management and tracking of contested litigation matters with milestone monitoring, discovery oversight, and vendor accountability across the full litigation lifecycle.

  • Discovery interrogatory review and documentation support
  • Litigation fee reviews and compliance tracking
  • Escalation management and impediment resolution

Eviction Oversight

Coordination of post-foreclosure eviction proceedings to ensure timely property possession and compliance.

  • Post-sale eviction timeline management
  • Vendor coordination and compliance tracking
  • Cash-for-keys program oversight

Title Curative

Cradle-to-grave monitoring of title issues spanning senior lien clearance, repurchases, mobile home resolution, legal description defects, origination issues, and title claims.

  • Issue identification through foreclosure counsel notification
  • Review, validation, and grading of title issues
  • Curative strategy based on risk, complexity, and jurisdiction
  • Active monitoring against historical curative timelines
  • Resolution, clearance, and file progression

Claims Oversight

End-to-end visibility, compliance, and control across the claims lifecycle covering FHA, FNMA, FHLMC, MI, VA, USDA, and eviction-related claims.

  • Claims deadline monitoring and compliance tracking
  • FHA Part A & B, FNMA 571, MI claims, and rebuttals
  • Initial, final, and supplemental claims filing status
  • Loss analysis and post-mortem reviews
  • Claims performance analytics and custom reporting

Loss Mitigation

Comprehensive oversight of loss mitigation strategies aligned with client objectives and borrower outcomes, spanning both retention and resolution approaches.

  • Borrower-retention: mediation and loan modifications
  • Resolution-driven: short sales, deeds in lieu, cash-for-keys
  • Data-driven strategy support for client initiatives

FTE Staffing & Capacity Management
