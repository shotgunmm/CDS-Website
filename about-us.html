About Us

Built on Oversight.
Driven by Results.

Since 2010, Consolidated Default Services has been the trusted oversight partner for mortgage servicers and financial institutions managing complex default portfolios.

Our Story

CDS was founded in 2010 with a straightforward premise: mortgage servicers needed more than a referral network. They needed someone to manage what happened after the referral — to make sure files moved, deadlines were met, and vendors were held accountable across dozens of jurisdictions simultaneously.

We built that capability through direct dialogue with our clients. The problems they described weren't unique to one servicer; they were systemic. Fragmented vendor networks. Inconsistent timelines. Compliance gaps that only surfaced at audit. No single point of accountability. We heard it repeatedly, and we built the answer.

The result was a formal oversight framework spanning foreclosure, bankruptcy, litigation, loss mitigation, title, and claims — the foundation of what CDS does today. What began as a support function has grown into a full-spectrum oversight practice trusted by some of the nation's largest servicers and portfolio managers.

Our Mission

To provide comprehensive, transparent, and accountable oversight of mortgage default services — reducing operational burden, mitigating risk, and enabling our clients to focus on strategic priorities while we ensure execution, compliance, and performance across their portfolios.

"The servicers we work with are managing enormous complexity across dozens of vendors and jurisdictions. Our job is to make that invisible to them — to ensure every file is moving, every deadline is met, and every exception is caught before it becomes a problem."

— CDS Leadership

What Sets Us Apart

Our Approach to Oversight

01

Proactive, Not Reactive

We identify issues before they become problems. Exception-based monitoring catches delays, compliance gaps, and vendor performance issues in real time. Structured 15- and 30-day follow-ups ensure SLA adherence and increase resolution velocity.

02

National Coverage, Local Expertise

Coverage across all 50 states and 94 U.S. Bankruptcy Courts with continuous collection of national and local rules — including trustee, jurisdictional, and state-specific requirements and regulatory updates.

03

Technology-Enabled Transparency

Proprietary firm performance scorecarding leveraging multiple data sources to automatically generate firm-level metrics. Monthly centralized reporting covering file grades, aging, and attorney performance.
